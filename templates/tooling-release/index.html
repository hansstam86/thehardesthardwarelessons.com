Tooling release checklist

The questions to answer before you cut steel. After release, a change is measured in weeks and tens of thousands of euros.

Before you release hard tooling

  • Thermal has been simulated and then measured on real hardware under worst-case conditions.
  • Tolerances in the drawings can be held by a real production process (checked with the mould maker).
  • The assembly sequence was walked through with the factory, step by step, with no manual rescue steps.
  • Every component in the design can be sourced at volume, not just for prototypes.
  • EMC pre-scan has been done on hardware that matches the final layout.
  • Fit and function are proven on production-intent parts, not hand-made ones.
  • The design is frozen: changes after this point need written approval and a cost estimate.
  • The cost of a change after release is written down and signed off by the person paying.
  • A named person has signed: "I would stake my own budget on this release."

Examples from the book
